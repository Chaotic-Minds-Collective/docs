# Three divisions. One collective.

Chaotic Minds Collective is organized around three distinct divisions. Each has a clear area of focus, while sharing the parent organization's identity and operating principles.

- **[Chaotic Creations](creations.html)** - The creative home for ideas, projects, experiments, and future ventures.
- **[Chaotic Solutions](solutions.html)** - Website development, technical services, support, security reviews, and documentation.
- **[Chaotic Gaming](gaming.html)** - Gaming communities, multiplayer servers, websites, tools, and community experiences.

## How the divisions relate

The divisions are not interchangeable departments. They exist to keep different kinds of work organized and recognizable:

- Creations provides a home for original ideas and ventures as they take shape.
- Solutions delivers practical technical work and services.
- Gaming focuses on communities and experiences built around games.

A project belongs in the division that best matches its purpose. Cross-division collaboration is possible when a project genuinely needs it, but a shared brand does not automatically make every project a shared product.

## Shared identity

The divisions use a recognizable family identity built around dark foundations and electric cyan or turquoise accents. Each division can develop its own emblem and supporting visual details while remaining connected to the Collective.
