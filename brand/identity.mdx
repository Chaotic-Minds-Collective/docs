# Brand identity

Chaotic Minds Collective uses a recognizable family identity across its parent organization and divisions. The visual direction is unconventional, creative, dark, technical, and commercially credible.

## Shared visual foundation

The core palette is built around:

- **Black and charcoal** for the foundation.
- **Electric cyan and turquoise** for the defining accent.
- Supporting neutral tones where they improve contrast, hierarchy, and readability.

The goal is a coherent family of brands, not three unrelated identities.

## Original visual language

Skulls, circuitry, technology, and distinctive original motifs are part of the visual direction. Artwork should feel intentional and recognizable rather than generic or carelessly generated.

Use original assets or assets with appropriate licenses. Names, logos, fonts, slogans, and artwork should not be assumed legally cleared merely because they appear in a concept or draft.

## Division-level expression

Each division may use its own emblem, supporting motifs, and additional accent details while retaining the Collective's shared foundation.

- **Creations:** creative expression, original artwork, and experimental ideas.
- **Solutions:** technical clarity, capability, and practical problem-solving.
- **Gaming:** community, play, and gaming culture.

## Applying the identity

When creating public-facing material, preserve readability, contrast, consistency, and the integrity of approved artwork. Do not stretch, redraw, recolor, or repurpose official marks in ways that undermine the identity.

This page describes the current brand direction. It does not replace an approved asset library or legal clearance review.
