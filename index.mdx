# Welcome to Chaotic Minds Collective

**A home for unconventional ideas, practical solutions, and communities built with purpose.**

Chaotic Minds Collective brings together creative work, technical services, and gaming communities under one shared identity. Each division has its own focus. Together, they make room for ideas to become useful projects, services, and experiences.

## One identity, room for individuality

Our shared visual foundation uses black, charcoal, and electric cyan or turquoise, supported by original artwork and distinctive motifs. Each division can develop its own character without losing its connection to the Collective.

## A living handbook

This handbook is the public reference for who we are, what our divisions do, and the principles that guide our work. Project details and availability can change, so consult the relevant project documentation for current scope and status.

**Ideas are not automatically commitments. Prototypes are not releases. Plans are not proof of implementation.** We aim to make those distinctions clear throughout our work.
