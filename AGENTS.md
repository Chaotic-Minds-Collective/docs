# Chaotic Minds Collective Handbook

## About this project

This repository contains the public organizational handbook for Chaotic Minds Collective. It is not an employee handbook, HR portal, internal engineering runbook, or product API reference.

The handbook explains the organization, its divisions, selected projects, public brand identity, and public-facing operating principles.

## Organization and terminology

- **Chaotic Minds Collective** is the parent organization.
- **Chaotic Creations** is the creative home for ideas, projects, experiments, and future ventures.
- **Chaotic Solutions** provides website development and redesign, IT support, security and leak reviews, maintenance, bug fixes, and documentation. Its tagline is **The Method to Your Madness.**
- **Chaotic Gaming** focuses on gaming communities, multiplayer servers, gaming websites and tools, community management, and future automation. Its tagline is **For Gamers, By Gamers.**
- **The Diagnostic Bay** is a separate automotive diagnostic website project under the broader organization. Maintain its own scope, roadmap, technical requirements, and approval process.

Do not treat an idea as an approved product, business, division, or release unless the project record confirms that status.

## Writing and style

- Write in clear, direct English.
- Use concise headings and explain specialized terms when needed.
- Keep the tone unconventional, creative, and technically credible without becoming generic corporate copy.
- Preserve the organization's terminology and approved slogans.
- Distinguish verified facts, proposals, assumptions, and unknowns.
- Do not invent staff, departments, benefits, compensation, service guarantees, features, launch dates, or policies.
- Do not use em dashes or en dashes as punctuation in new content.
- Use Mintlify components where they improve navigation or comprehension.

## Public content boundaries

This is a public handbook. Do not publish credentials, API keys, private information, client details, confidential project records, access instructions, sensitive security findings, or internal operational secrets.

Document public-facing principles rather than private infrastructure details. Keep project-specific technical documentation in the appropriate project repository or controlled workspace.

## Approval and status discipline

The Owner and Operator retains final decision-making authority. Planning, research, drafting, and recommendations do not constitute approval to spend, purchase, publish, deploy, or make irreversible changes.

Clearly distinguish proposed, approved, implemented, tested, and deployed work. A plan or prototype is not proof of a production release.

## Brand identity

Use the shared family identity: black, charcoal, and electric cyan or turquoise. Original skull, circuitry, and technology motifs may be used where appropriate. Divisions can have distinct emblems and supporting accents while retaining a coherent parent-brand connection.

Use original or properly licensed assets. Do not assume names, logos, fonts, slogans, or artwork are legally cleared.

## Quality checks

Before proposing a handbook change, check factual accuracy, navigation, links, accessibility, privacy, security, costs where relevant, and consistency with the approved organization structure. Keep the live handbook unchanged until the Owner and Operator approves publication.
