# Public handbook brief

## Description

The Chaotic Minds Collective handbook is the public organizational reference for the parent organization, its three divisions, selected projects, shared brand identity, and public-facing operating principles.

## Primary audience

People who want to understand Chaotic Minds Collective, what its divisions do, what it is building, and how the organization approaches its work.

## Jobs to be done

- Understand the Collective's purpose and organizational structure.
- Distinguish Creations, Solutions, and Gaming.
- Discover selected projects without mistaking concepts or prototypes for released products.
- Understand the shared visual identity and division-level expression.
- Understand public-facing principles for ownership, approvals, project status, security, and documentation.

## Motivation

The existing Mintlify site contained generic employee onboarding, HR, salary, policy, and engineering template material that did not describe this organization. The handbook is being rebuilt as a genuine public organizational reference.

## Content boundaries

This is not an HR portal or an internal runbook. Exclude credentials, private information, client records, confidential project details, sensitive security findings, and internal access procedures.

## Assumptions to verify

- The handbook is intended for public readers, not only collaborators.
- The existing repository logo assets and dark cyan visual direction are the intended starting point.
- Project status must be confirmed in project-specific records; handbook summaries should avoid implying that a concept is released.
